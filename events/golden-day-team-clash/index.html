Team Recap

Golden Day Pickleball Team Clash

August 31, 2026Kawit, Cavite

A tough day on the draw, but a genuinely good day for the team. OCPC came to compete at the Golden Day Pickleball Team Clash and came home closer for it.

We Showed Up Together

OCPC did not make the elimination round this time. That part stung—but it never became the whole story. The day was full of shared rallies, sideline support, new experiences under pressure, and the kind of laughter that makes a competition feel like a club day too.

More Than the Result

Every event gives the club something to take forward. For this one, it was a stronger bond, more confidence in each other, and another reminder that showing up as a team matters. We will take the fun, the lessons, and the energy into the next one.

OCPC takeaway: no elimination berth, but plenty of good memories and an even tighter crew.
