Club Recap

OCPC Goes to Laguna!

August 30, 2026Bad Pickle, Carmona · The Pickle & Co. 1016, Calamba

One Saturday, two stops, plenty of rallies. The crew began close to home in Carmona before crossing into Laguna for a late-night second session in Calamba.

First Stop: Bad Pickle

The day opened at Bad Pickle in Carmona, Cavite—an easy first stop for the group to get the games going, settle into the day, and share the court with familiar faces.

Then On to Calamba

When the first session wrapped, the day was only halfway done. OCPC headed to The Pickle & Co. 1016 in Calamba, Laguna, where the games carried on until midnight. That is the kind of itinerary this club knows how to keep: play, eat, travel, repeat.

Two courts, one long Saturday: Bad Pickle in Carmona, then The Pickle & Co. 1016 in Calamba—ending the night with the lights still on and the paddles still out.
