June 28, 2026 · SM City Rosario

Rainbow Rally 2026

🏳️‍🌈 Pickleball Pride Fest 🤝 With Prestige Paddle PH

One Cavite Pickleball Club hosted Rainbow Rally 2026, a one-day Pride-themed sports festival held at SM City Rosario in partnership with Prestige Paddle PH. The event ran genderless doubles categories for both novice and intermediate players, built around an inclusive, community-first spirit — open to LGBTQIA+ players, allies, and anyone who wanted to play. A great turnout and an even better vibe — thank you to everyone who came out to celebrate and compete.
