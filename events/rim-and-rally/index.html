Club Invitation

OCPC at Rim and Rally Sports Hub

September 27, 2026Dasmariñas, Cavite

Rim and Rally Sports Hub invited OCPC for an exclusive club session, and the crew gladly made its way to Dasmariñas for another day of connection through the game.

An Invitation Worth Taking

There is something especially good about a club invitation: another venue opening its doors, players coming together with no pressure beyond enjoying the game, and a new court becoming part of the community map.

OCPC appreciates the welcome from Rim and Rally Sports Hub. More court visits mean more friendships, more rallies, and more reasons for the club to keep moving.

Thank you, Rim and Rally: for the invitation and for making OCPC feel at home in Dasmariñas.
