Club Invitation

OCPC at ALAB Pickleball Court

October 2, 2026Imus, Cavite

ALAB Pickleball Court welcomed OCPC to Imus for a night of play, laughs, and the kind of club energy that makes every invitation feel like a reunion.

Hosted With Heart

There is always something special about being invited to a new court: a warm welcome, new rallies, and a chance for the squad to share the game with another part of the local pickleball community. ALAB Pickleball Court gave OCPC exactly that in Imus.

The session brought the crew together for plenty of play and good company. It was one of those nights where the fun does not stop at the last point.

One More Stop Together

After the courts, the whole squad headed to McDonald's to eat, catch up, and keep the night going. The food was simple, but the time together made the stop feel special—heart-filling in the best way.

Thank you, ALAB Pickleball Court: for hosting OCPC and making the squad feel so welcome in Imus.
