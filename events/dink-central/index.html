OCPC Goes to Dink Central PH

September 20, 2026Silang, Cavite

One day after the dual meet, the club was back on court—this time for a visit to Dink Central PH in Silang. A new setting, familiar people, and plenty more play.

Another Court, Same Club Energy

OCPC road trips are never just about where the court is. They are about getting people together, meeting more of the local pickleball community, and turning a regular session into a shared memory. Dink Central PH was the next stop on that list.

Next stop logged: Dink Central PH, Silang—another Cavite court added to the OCPC road-trip board.
