Road Trip

OCPC Goes to Lipa & Santa Rosa!

September 29, 2026Rallyhaus · Lipa City · Court Zero · Santa Rosa

A two-city paddle day: first to Rallyhaus in Lipa City, Batangas, then back toward Laguna for Court Zero in Santa Rosa. OCPC made the most of the road.

Lipa: Rallyhaus

The first stop was Rallyhaus in Lipa City. The group brought the OCPC banner, filled the court with good energy, and got to share the day with another growing pickleball community.

Santa Rosa: Court Zero

Then it was on to Court Zero in Santa Rosa, Laguna for the second leg. Different courts, same group, and more games to close out a long but very good day.

Two cities, two venues, one club: Rallyhaus in Lipa City and Court Zero in Santa Rosa made for an OCPC road trip worth remembering.
