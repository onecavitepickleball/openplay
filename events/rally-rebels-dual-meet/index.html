OCPC × Rally Rebels Dual Meet

September 19, 2026Rally District PH · Kawit, Cavite

A full house, a full day of matches, and a meeting of two clubs that came ready to play. The OCPC × Rally Rebels Dual Meet brought the competition and the community together at Rally District PH.

Two Clubs, One Match Day

OCPC and Rally Rebels filled Rally District PH in Kawit with team colors, paddle taps, and a packed match-day atmosphere. From the first warm-ups to the last calls at court, the day had the energy of a proper club-versus-club meet.

Competition, Then Community

The fixtures gave everyone something to play for, but the best part was the feeling around the venue: players supporting their pairs, teams cheering courtside, and both clubs sharing a good day of pickleball. This is the kind of meet we want more of.

Thank you, Rally Rebels: for meeting OCPC at the net and helping make the first dual meet one to remember.
