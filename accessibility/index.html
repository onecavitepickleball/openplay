Accessibility Help.

We want every OCPC member — regardless of ability — to be able to check the schedule, join a session, and follow the club online.

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Our Commitment

We're a community-run club site, built and maintained on an ongoing basis. We aim to follow established web accessibility practices so the site works well with keyboards, screen readers, and a range of devices — and we treat accessibility issues as bugs worth fixing.

What We've Built In

  • Descriptive alt text on player photos, sponsor logos, and product images.
  • A responsive layout that adapts from desktop down to small phone screens.
  • Support for reduced motion — animated elements like our homepage sponsor strip respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, forms, and interactive tools (like the Rules & Etiquette page's court diagrams and scoring simulators).
  • Clear, high-contrast text set in a readable type scale across the site.

Known Limitations

Some interactive pieces — like the SVG-based court diagrams and scoring simulators on our Rules & Etiquette page — are still being refined for screen reader support. If you run into something that doesn't work well with your assistive technology, please tell us; that feedback directly shapes what we fix next.

Browser & Device Support

The site is built to work on current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, on both desktop and mobile. If a page looks broken on your setup, try refreshing or updating your browser first — and let us know if the issue persists.

Get in Touch

If any part of this site is difficult to use because of a disability, we want to hear about it. Message us on Facebook or through our Contact page and describe the issue and the device/browser/assistive tech you were using — we'll do our best to fix it or find another way to help.

Tip: Most modern phones and computers also have their own built-in accessibility tools (screen readers, text scaling, high-contrast mode, reduce-motion settings) that work alongside this site's own support.
