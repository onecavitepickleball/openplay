Club Trip Recap

Two Days in Zambales.

📅 July 25–26, 2026 📍 Zambales & Pampanga 🤝 With Zambales Pickleball Club & Court Rebels Club

Some trips end the moment the van pulls back into Cavite. This one didn't, not really. Two full days, one packed itinerary, and a whole lot of OCPC energy later, we're still replaying it in the group chat.

One Cavite Pickleball Club · The Highgrounds Compound, Iba, Zambales

Before Sunrise

Call time was 3AM. We left Cavite at 4AM. So no, the trip didn't start loud, it started quiet. Half the van was asleep before we even hit the highway, running on pure willpower and whatever snacks were within reach. That's just what a 4AM departure does to a group of grown adults who play pickleball for fun.

But somewhere between the sunrise and the first few stopovers, everyone woke back up, and that's when the trip actually began. Conversations got louder, snacks got passed around properly, and kuya Mhon's dad jokes started landing, each one somehow funnier than the last for how little sense they made. By the time we reached Zambales, the sleepy van from four hours earlier was long gone. We rolled into Liwliwa, San Felipe, our home base for the trip, wide awake and ready.

Somewhere past sunrise, wide awake again
Arrival in Liwliwa, San Felipe, where the Airbnb was based

The Highgrounds Compound, Iba

Day one was all about open play with Zambales Pickleball Club at The Highgrounds Compound in Iba: new faces, good matches, and courts we don't get to play on nearly enough. It's also where one of the trip's best running jokes was born.

Running Joke of the Trip During one of the matches, Des accidentally hit Jerome square in the chest with the ball, and that was all the rest of OCPC needed. From that point on it was open season: cupid had struck, Jerome was declared officially unable to escape, and the two of them got teased and shipped relentlessly for the rest of the trip. As is tradition.
OCPC & Zambales Pickleball Club, The Highgrounds Compound

Airbnb Nights

After a full day on the courts, we wound down at our Airbnb, and this is where mommy Nene gave what can only be described as an Oscar-worthy performance. She pulled out that TikTok trend where you say the same line in a bunch of different emotions, and ran through "matutulog ako" as Angry, Disappointed, Flirty, and Happy, each one more unhinged than the last. We were in tears laughing. Add in a pool, good company, and zero urgency to be anywhere, and that's how a night at the Airbnb turns into one of the best parts of the whole trip.

Pool bonding, Casa Kymani

Day Two: Court Hopping to Pampanga

We left Zambales at 12:30PM on July 26 and drove straight for Pampanga, where Day 2 open play was waiting. On the way, we made a quick stop at Jungle Base Pickleball for coffee and photos at their courts before continuing on, a nice little detour on the way to the main event.

Coffee stop, Jungle Base Pickleball

Then it was on to the Pampanga Pickleball Center for Day 2 open play, where we got to share the courts with Court Rebels Club for a full round of games.

Open play, Pampanga Pickleball Center
Good games, better company, with Court Rebels Club
A huge thank you to Court Rebels Club for the games at Pampanga Pickleball Center. The matches were competitive and the welcome was even better. That's really what these trips are about: showing up in someone else's city and leaving as friends.

The Never-Dying Adobo

Nobody left this trip hungry. Dinner was adobo, inihaw na liempo, and bbq, the kind of home-cooked, still-warm food that makes a long day of pickleball worth it. The adobo, specifically, deserves its own headline: it was so good and so plentiful that it survived into the next day and was still being eaten well into Day 2. Truly, the never-dying adobo. Huge thanks to Ria for cooking it, and to Marvin for prepping the bbq, with the boys taking over grill duty to cook it and the liempo to smoky perfection. Our unofficial sous chefs mommy Nene, Des, Vhen Vhen, and kuya Mhon kept breakfast running, and Jerome pulled dishwasher duty the whole trip. A whole crew keeping everyone fed.

And then there was the snacking: non-stop, all trip. Roadside tupig passed around straight from the van, and of course Kaka Coco's ice cream served in an actual coconut shell, which is exactly as good as it sounds.

Kaka Coco, and a roadside snack run

We did get scammed on a food tray dinner order at one point (we won't name names, but we see you). Honestly, though? It barely put a dent in the trip. We laughed it off, added it to the group chat lore, and kept going. Throw in a nonstop sound trip in the van and whatever chaos was happening courtside at any given moment, and that's the trip in a nutshell.

Worth Every Minute

Two full days, spent well, with people who can turn even a scammed dinner order into a good story. That's OCPC in a nutshell. Thank you to everyone who came along, to Zambales Pickleball Club and Team Highlands for a great night on the courts, and to Court Rebels Club for the games in Pampanga. A special thank you to Ria and Joe for orchestrating both meetups from start to finish, none of this happens without you two. Until the next trip and the next round of court hopping, we're only getting stronger.
