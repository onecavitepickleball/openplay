Privacy Notice.

A short, plain-language notice of what we collect at the moments that matter — signing up and ordering gear — and your rights over that data.

Last updated: July 29, 2026

What This Notice Covers

This notice is a quick summary, shown alongside the points where you actually hand over information — creating a player profile or placing a merch order. For the full picture of how we handle data across the site, see our Privacy Policy.

At Sign-Up / Profile Creation

When you create a player profile, we collect your name, email, and (optionally) a profile photo, used to identify you on the leaderboard, queue, and birthday list, and to let you manage your own profile.

At Checkout (Merch Orders)

When you order a uniform or shirt, we collect your name, contact details, size/color choice, and payment reference number — used only to fulfill and confirm that specific order.

Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

  • Right to be informed — knowing what's collected and why (this notice).
  • Right to access — asking what data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct — fixing inaccurate profile or order details.
  • Right to erasure — requesting we delete your profile or order data, subject to any records we need to keep for completed orders.
  • Right to object — opting out of non-essential data uses, like analytics.

How to Exercise These Rights

Message us on Facebook or through our Contact page with your request, and we'll get back to you directly.

See also: our full Privacy Policy for details on our service providers (Firebase, Cloudinary, Google Analytics, GCash) and how data is stored and secured.
