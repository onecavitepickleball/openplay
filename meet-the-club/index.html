Meet the Club.

Who we are, the players and organizers who make OCPC what it is, and answers to common questions.

One Cavite Pickleball Club · SM City Rosario

One Cavite Pickleball Club (OCPC) is a community of players in Cavite who gather for open play sessions every week — no tryouts, no fixed teams, just a rotating group that shows up to play.

Whether you've never held a paddle or you're chasing your next tournament, our open play format mixes skill levels so everyone gets real games and real reps. Regulars help newcomers pick up the rules fast, and the group has grown mostly by word of mouth and a very active Facebook page.

We keep things simple: check the weekly schedule, register your session, show up with a paddle (or borrow one), and rotate onto a court.

First-timers at OCPC pay a ₱200 open play fee, regardless of skill level. After your first session, the fee drops to ₱100 per person — good for the whole session. First-timers are encouraged to pay as they join, preferably via GCash. Once you're a regular, fees can be settled on-site.

It's best to bring your own if you have one, but paddle rental is available on-site for ₱50 if you're just trying it out.

Non-marking court shoes, comfortable athletic wear, and your own water — courts get warm and there isn't always a store nearby. Our sublimation uniform is optional but a nice way to rep the club — check the Shop page.
