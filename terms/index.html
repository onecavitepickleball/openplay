02About OCPC

OCPC is a community-run recreational pickleball club based in Cavite, Philippines, organizing open play sessions (primarily at SM City Rosario), occasional tournaments and outings, and selling limited-run club merchandise. OCPC is not a registered corporation, sports federation, or franchise of any national or international pickleball body — we're a group of players who organize sessions together.

03Membership & Eligibility

Open play sessions are open to players of all skill levels. There's no membership fee to attend open play as a guest, but creating a player profile (via Join OCPC) is how you get listed on Meet the Club, appear on the Leaderboard, and receive a birthday shoutout.

  • You must provide accurate information when creating a player profile — name, birthday, contact details, and a real photo of yourself.
  • Junior players (under 18) may attend and sign up with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian, who is responsible for the junior's conduct and safety.
  • OCPC reserves the right to decline registration or attendance to anyone whose presence poses a safety or conduct risk to other players.

04Player Profiles & Accounts

Player profiles are authenticated through Firebase Authentication using the email and password you set at sign-up. You're responsible for:

  • Keeping your password confidential and not sharing your account with anyone else.
  • Keeping your profile information (name, birthday, photo, contact details) accurate and up to date.
  • Notifying us if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

Per our current process, a new profile shows as "pending" until an OCPC officer has recorded your attendance at open play 3 times — after that, your profile appears publicly on Meet the Club and you become eligible for a birthday shoutout. We may adjust this process over time.

05Open Play Sessions & Attendance

Open play schedules, venues, and times are posted on the Site and are subject to change — check the schedule before heading out, especially around holidays or weather disruptions. Registering interest in a session (e.g. through our registration link) does not guarantee a court slot; sessions run on a rotation and queue system managed on-site.

By attending a session, you agree to follow the Rules & Etiquette published on this Site, any additional instructions from the officer or organizer running that session, and the venue's own house rules (e.g. SM City Rosario's facility policies).

Please sit out and inform an officer if you feel unwell, dizzy, or injured during a session — don't push through pain.

08Merchandise Orders & Payments

OCPC sells club merchandise through the Shop page: add items to your cart, then complete payment on the Checkout page. This means:

  • Prices shown are per piece and are current at the time of ordering — we'll notify you of any change before your order is finalized if it isn't reflected on the Shop page.
  • Orders are placed directly through our Checkout page and are not automatically confirmed until payment (GCash or InstaPay/bank transfer via QR) is verified against your submitted reference number and, if provided, receipt screenshot.
  • The Sublimation Uniform is a batch order — we collect orders until a minimum headcount is reached, then place one bulk print run, with turnaround typically 3–4 weeks after a batch closes. If a batch doesn't hit minimum within a reasonable time, we'll reach out about a refund or rolling your order into the next batch.
  • All other merchandise (caps, mugs, tumblers, and non-uniform shirts) is fulfilled individually, typically within 2–3 days of payment verification — no minimum headcount needed.

09Refunds & Cancellations

Because merchandise is produced per batch to order, we generally do not offer refunds once a batch has gone to print, except in cases of a manufacturing defect, wrong size/color sent due to our error, or an item that never arrives. If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible — cancellations are only possible before the batch you're part of has closed and gone to print.

By attending a session or event, you consent to being photographed or filmed as part of the group, and to that media being posted publicly, unless you tell an officer in advance that you'd prefer not to be included — we'll do our best to accommodate that request, though we can't guarantee exclusion from wide group/crowd shots.

Profile photos you upload when signing up are used specifically for your player profile, Meet the Club listing, and birthday shoutouts, and are stored via Cloudinary (see our Privacy Policy for details).

11Code of Conduct & Suspension

We want OCPC to be a welcoming, respectful space on and off the court. In addition to the on-court etiquette in our Rules & Etiquette page, all players and members are expected to:

  • Treat other players, organizers, and venue staff with respect — no harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.
  • Play fair and call lines honestly.
  • Follow reasonable instructions from session organizers regarding rotations, safety, or venue rules.
  • Represent the club respectfully when wearing OCPC merchandise or posting about OCPC on social media.

OCPC reserves the right to warn, temporarily suspend, or permanently remove any player from sessions, the roster, or this Site for conduct that violates this section — including unsafe play, harassment, repeated disruptive behavior, or misuse of club branding. Where practical, we'll give a warning first; serious incidents (safety threats, harassment) may result in immediate removal.
