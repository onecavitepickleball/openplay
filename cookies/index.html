Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains the browser storage and optional third-party content used by onecavitepickleball.club. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

Essential storage

We use browser storage that is needed for features you request, such as keeping cart items, remembering display preferences, and remembering your privacy choice. These items are not used for advertising.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is optional. It is off unless you press “Allow optional analytics” in the privacy notice. If allowed, it helps us understand general site use. You can change your choice any time through .

Third-party embeds

The Facebook feed and Google Map do not load until you choose to load them. Once loaded, their providers may set or read their own cookies and handle data under their own policies. External links, including Facebook and Sports360, are governed by those services when you open them.

Your choices

Rejecting optional analytics will not prevent you from using the core OCPC site. You can also clear browser storage in your browser settings. Clearing it may reset your cart, preferences, or saved privacy choice.
